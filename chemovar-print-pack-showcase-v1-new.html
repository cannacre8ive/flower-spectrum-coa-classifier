The Premise
The old labels don't tell you how it'll feel.
"Indica means sleepy, sativa means awake, hybrid is somewhere in between."
After 50+ years of crossbreeding, sativa and indica describe what the plant looks like — not what the flower does to you. Two flowers labeled the same way can land you in completely different headspaces.
What actually shapes the feel:
The unique mix of terpenes in every batch. Terpenes are the molecules behind the smell. They're also the molecules behind the feel. The same compounds that make lemon peel smell bright are the ones that lift your mood. The ones in lavender are the ones that help you wind down.
We track sixteen terpenes on every flower. Eight that drive most of the experience, eight more that fine-tune it. Open the brochure to see how they map.

Read the Fingerprint
Every flower has a chemical signature.
Sixteen molecules. Eight primary terpenes at the cardinal positions, eight secondary terpenes filling the diagonals. The further out a spoke pokes, the more of that terpene the flower contains.
Reading exampleDurban Poison · Flow
What you're reading:
Long spokes = dominant terpenes — they drive how the flower feels.
Cluster patterns = aromatic family — citrus terpenes group together, pine terpenes group together, floral terpenes group together.
Unusual bumps = the rare secondaries that make a flower distinctive even within its category.

Co-brand System

Every piece carries the Cannacre8ive system mark and the implementing dispensary's wordmark or logo lockup. The relationship is "powered by" — the dispensary brand is the primary identity on customer-facing pieces; the Cannacre8ive credit sits subordinate.

Dispensary placement
Primary identity. Wordmark or logo lockup, sage or cream color, sized for legibility at arm's length. Appears on every piece in the lower band.
Cannacre8ive credit
"Powered by Cannacre8ive · The Chemovar Guide v1.0" — DM Mono uppercase, cream-dim, smallest type in the layout. Dofollow link in digital versions.
QR target
Per-dispensary subdomain (e.g., bandos.chemovar.guide) lands on the consumer guide pre-configured for that store's current inventory. Default fallback: cannacre8ive.com/guide.
