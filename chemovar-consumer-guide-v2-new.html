01 — The Premise

The old labels don't tell you how it'll feel.

Sativa and indica describe what the plant looks like, not what the flower does to you. After 50+ years of crossbreeding, those old shelf labels have almost nothing to do with your actual experience. Two flowers labeled "indica" can land you in completely different headspaces.

"Indica means sleepy, sativa means awake, hybrid is somewhere in between."
What actually shapes how it feels is the terpene fingerprint — the unique mix of aromatic compounds in every batch.
Terpenes are the molecules behind the smell. They're also the molecules behind the feel. The same terpenes that make lemon peel smell bright are the ones that lift your mood. The ones that make lavender smell calming are the ones that help you wind down. There are sixteen we track on every flower — eight that drive most of the experience, eight more that fine-tune it. Together they form a chemical signature you can read at a glance.

07 — The Sixteen Molecules

The smell is the feel.

Terpenes are the aromatic compounds plants make to attract pollinators and ward off pests. They're also the molecules that shape how flowers feel. The first eight do most of the work; the second eight are the fine-tuning that makes one Caryophyllene-led flower feel different from the next.

If you've ever felt awake from peeling an orange, calm from cutting fresh lavender, or grounded from walking through pine trees — you've already experienced terpenes. The same molecules that make those smells are doing real work in your flower.
